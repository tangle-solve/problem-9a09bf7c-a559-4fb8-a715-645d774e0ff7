### CHEM-000559: Suppressing parasitic oxygen chemistry in rechargeable Li–O₂ cells
- **Subfield:** Electrochemical energy-storage chemistry
- **Open:** Cycle oxygen reduction and evolution with complete oxygen and charge balances while preventing singlet-oxygen damage to electrolyte, carbon and binder.
- **Status:** Rechargeability has been problematic since early nonaqueous Li–O₂ research. A 2025 study established Marcus-kinetic control of singlet/triplet oxygen production from superoxide, providing a possible intervention.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** It would improve access to the chemistry’s high theoretical specific energy.
- **Key works:** S. Mondal et al. (2025), *Marcus kinetics control singlet and triplet oxygen evolving from superoxide*, [DOI:10.1038/s41586-025-09587-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09587-7).
- **Related:** Photophysics: spin-selective reactive-oxygen generation. Materials science: oxidative interface stability.
